## Proposal

has been submitted to AIctiveInvest for consideration an funding:

TrAIvel Friend is an AI-based travel companion. It organizes the entire journey ("the experience") for the user, from the first vague idea of a destination, the booking of flights, hotel, theatre or museum tickets and restaurant tables to the processing of claims and the feded-back/recommendation for other travellers.

It is based on a personal agent
---

## Features

### Brainstorming about possible destinations, including a recommendation function based on purchases on other apps (for example Amazon or Opodo) and select personal preferences (sport, culture etc.)

### Pre-booking of flights and hotels for a selected time period and subject to final approval by the traveller.

### Reservation of restaurant tables, taxi or alternative transport as well as payment based on the traveller's credit card details (confirmation by traveller needed)

### Individual itineraries for each day (and night) and the destination.

### Personal agent who can translate requests into the local language and the answers back into the language of the traveler
