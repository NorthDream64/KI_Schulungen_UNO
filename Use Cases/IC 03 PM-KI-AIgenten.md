## 1 · Proposal

**PM-AIgent** is an AI-based, multi-skill project management system that replaces most, if not all aspects of traditional project management. Companies will be able - with the help of their PM-Agent to plan, staff, conduct, monitor and implement complex projects or programmes with only minimal human oversiht

## 2 · Product / Features

- **Optimize** PM-AIgent uses a specialiszed data base of all your past and current projects to draw "lessons learned" and recommend improvements in the way projects and portfolios are managed in your company; projects can be prioritized given stated strategic company objectives. 
- **Plan** with access to the PM-AIgent skills data base of your internal and external resources, the system can automatically suggest optimal allocation of resources, including changes to availability according to project priority and semi-automatic assignment by managers
- **Scenario planning** ...
- **Documentation** records meetings, e-mail
- **Assess** conduct interviews, market research
- **Implement* develop training and adoption initatives

All features are available in English and German as a standard. Further language by request at extra cost.
